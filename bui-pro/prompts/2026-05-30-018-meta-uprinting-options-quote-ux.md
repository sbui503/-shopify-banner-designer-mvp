Role: Meta AI UX/research partner.

Reference researched: https://www.uprinting.com/custom-sticker-printing.html

Product: BUI Banner Pro mobile-first cannabis sticker, label, packaging design and quote tool.

UX task: Build a clean ordering/quote flow modeled on public UPrinting custom sticker options, but branded as BUI Banner Pro.

Recommendations:
- Keep Design as the first mobile shortcut.
- Place shape presets and custom shape upload inside the Design/Templates area.
- Put custom size controls near the size catalog.
- Quote panel should be compact and novice-friendly: sticker type, shape, size, material, finish, white ink/material treatment, bundling, quantity.
- Use material cards/filters that visibly change the design preview so customers understand white paper vs clear, metallic, holographic, kraft, vinyl/BOPP.
- Keep price as “BUI estimate” and show “includes 20% service margin” instead of naming competitor pricing in the UI.
- Do not overload the canvas with explanatory text.
